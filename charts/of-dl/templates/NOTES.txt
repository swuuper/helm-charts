OF-DL is installed as a CronJob running on schedule {{ .Values.schedule | quote }}.

Downloads are written to {{ .Values.config.DownloadPath | default (dig "Download" "DownloadPath" "/data" .Values.config) }} inside the pod -- make sure a volume is
mounted there via `volumes` / `volumeMounts`, otherwise everything lands on the
pod's ephemeral filesystem and is lost when the job finishes.

Trigger a run without waiting for the schedule:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} create job --from=cronjob/{{ include "of-dl.fullname" . }}-run-download {{ include "of-dl.fullname" . }}-manual

Then follow it:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -f job/{{ include "of-dl.fullname" . }}-manual
